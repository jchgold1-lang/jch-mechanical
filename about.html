About JCH Mechanical

Professional HVAC service built on honesty, quality workmanship, and customer satisfaction.

Your Local HVAC Professional

My name is Jaykob Hill, and I am the founder of JCH Mechanical. Throughout my career in the HVAC industry, I have personally helped serve over 3,500 homes throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth area while working with multiple HVAC companies.


After graduating high school in 2024, I attended Tulsa Welding School where I completed their HVAC/R program. During my time in school, I began an apprenticeship in the field, gaining hands-on experience and developing the skills needed to provide quality HVAC service.


Today, I am a TDLR Certified HVAC Technician and hold my EPA 608 Universal Certification.


During my time in the industry, I noticed that the focus was often on who could sell the most. While growth is important, I believe the key to success is taking care of people.


At JCH Mechanical, we treat our clients like family. We provide honest service, fair pricing, reliable repairs, and quality workmanship on every job.


Whether you need a small repair, maintenance, or a complete system replacement, our goal is to provide the 5-star service your home deserves.
